## Parameters Description

### **Tones**

1. **Formal**: Professional and structured, suitable for business or academic settings.
2. **Casual**: Relaxed and conversational, like friends chatting.
3. **Humorous**: Witty and playful with jokes and light-hearted comments.
4. **Serious**: Focused and no-nonsense, often for critical discussions.
5. **Inspirational**: Motivational and uplifting, designed to encourage.
6. **Argumentative**: Debate-like, where participants discuss opposing views.
7. **Empathetic**: Supportive and understanding, ideal for counseling or personal topics.
8. **Neutral**: Objective and unbiased, suitable for reporting or informational exchanges.
9. **Romantic**: Sweet and affectionate, focusing on emotions.
10. **Sarcastic**: Use of irony and mockery in a clever way.
11. **Mystical**: Enigmatic, with a touch of fantasy or mystery.
12. **Technical**: Detailed and jargon-heavy, for engineering or scientific discussions.

---

### **Desired Lengths**

1. **Very Short**: 3-4 exchanges (e.g., quick Q&A).
2. **Short**: 5-7 exchanges (e.g., brief interaction or concise dialogue).
3. **Medium**: 8-12 exchanges (e.g., detailed conversation or scenario).
4. **Long**: 13-20 exchanges (e.g., full discussion or scene development).
5. **Very Long**: 20+ exchanges (e.g., in-depth debate or storytelling).

---

### **Styles**

1. **Friendly**: Warm and approachable, with informal language.
2. **Professional**: Structured and polished, for workplace or formal settings.
3. **Academic**: Analytical and precise, suitable for educational scenarios.
4. **Persuasive**: Convincing and rhetorical, focused on changing opinions.
5. **Storytelling**: Narrative-driven, with vivid descriptions and a natural flow.
6. **Role-playing**: Each participant has a defined persona (e.g., customer and support agent, teacher and student).
7. **Q&A**: Question and answer format (e.g., interview or FAQ).
8. **Philosophical**: Reflective and abstract, exploring deeper meanings or concepts.
9. **Mystery**: Suspenseful and cryptic, with clues and twists.
10. **Explanatory**: Step-by-step and descriptive, ideal for teaching or tutorials.
11. **Debate**: Back-and-forth argumentation, with logical reasoning and counterpoints.
12. **Playful**: Light-hearted and imaginative, like children’s storytelling or games.
13. **Customer Service**: Polite and problem-solving, mimicking real-world service scenarios.
14. **Historical**: Period-specific language and style, mimicking past eras.
15. **Fantasy**: Filled with imaginative elements, mythical creatures, and magic.
